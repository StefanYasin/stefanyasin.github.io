Questions people ask before we talk

What kind of businesses do you work with?
Small and mid-sized businesses in Hamilton, the GTA and across Ontario. Machine shops and plants with industrial IT, contractors with no in-house IT, and service businesses that want AI that answers customers without inventing things.
Can you take over infrastructure someone else installed?
That is most of the work. I document what is there, find what is fragile, and fix the parts that will cost you a weekend. Nothing gets ripped out to make room for a preferred brand.
Do you build AI that runs in production, or prototypes?
Production. Validation gates before a model output reaches a customer, logging so every conversation is replayable, and monitoring so you hear about problems before your customers do. I have run over 500,000 validated AI interactions across production pipelines.
Are you taking contract work, or a full time role?
Both. Cloud and Cable is the delivery business for client work, and I am open to senior in-house roles in Canada, remote, the United States or Europe.

Let us build something.

IT that is boring in the best way, AI that pays for itself, or infrastructure that outlasts the building. One conversation, no sales pitch.
